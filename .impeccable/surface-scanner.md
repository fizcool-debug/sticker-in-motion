# Scanner direction contract

THESIS: the camera and sticker lead; quiet controls provide actionable feedback.
OWN-WORLD: artwork-led collection, near-black ink, solid white controls, self-hosted Space Grotesk. Provisional app identity: Sticker in Motion. CET is a test sticker only.
STORY: enter camera immediately, recognize a supported sticker, show anchored motion; offer a separate accessible animation preview. Sticker names appear only when recognized or selected for preview.
FIRST VIEWPORT: full-screen camera, faint open-corner guide and one small preview control. No identity header or bottom panel. A four-second hint introduces scanning.
FORM: edge-to-edge camera in all orientations; safe-area floating controls; centered recovery only for loading/errors/pause. Guide vanishes on recognition; replay appears only when recognized. Preview remains a separate screen.
